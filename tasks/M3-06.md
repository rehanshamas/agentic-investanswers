## Plan
Standing approval from Sham (2026-11-13, "keep backlog thread busy"); one-line plan posted in the project thread.

All in `contexts/backtesting/src/domain/backtest.ts` (pure):
- Signals: `enter` (long or short, optional stop and target), `exit`, `protect` (move or remove the stop and target; trailing stops).
- Rules (TradingView Strategy Tester defaults): fill at the next open; one position, reversal on the other side's entry; stops and targets inside the bar, fill bar included, along TradingView's path (high nearer the open: open, high, low, close; else open, low, high, close), gaps fill at the open; percentage commission per fill; fixed or percent-of-equity sizing.
- `startBacktest`, `stepBacktest` (one bar, state unchanged), `summarise`, and `backtest` (all bars, built on `stepBacktest`). Client input via Sham (2026-11-13): a backtest can run "towards hydration" (all history at once) or "towards the bar drawings" (bar by bar as bars are drawn); both use `stepBacktest`, so they agree.

## Handoff
**Done.** Engine and 28 hand-worked tests: each Strategy Tester rule, totals, cent-exact sums, and bar-by-bar equal to the whole-history run at every one of 120 bars. State is plain data and survives a JSON round trip, so it can be cached like an indicator run. `pnpm check` green.

**Left.**
- Parity against TradingView's export of the SMA demo strategy (OI-30, added in this PR with the exact script). M3-03's harness compares trades and totals exactly.
- Wiring: an indicator turns its outputs into `Signal`s (M3-05 for the SMA demo, M3-07c for ATR). A server-side cache of backtest state, like the indicator engine's, when the chart shows live backtests.
- Not modelled (not TradingView defaults; add when an indicator needs them): pyramiding, slippage, partial exits, `process_orders_on_close`, margin calls.

**Surprises.** Totals are summed from per-trade profits rounded to the cent, as the Strategy Tester lists them; summing unrounded profits can differ by a cent. To confirm against the export.
