## Plan
**1. Data, kept as explicit lists per year** (as now, and as `docs/bar-rules.md` says: "one list per year"), so every date can be reviewed by eye.

**2. Where the dates come from: rules, written sources, and a cross-check against real data.**
- **Scheduled holidays from rules**, computed once by a script that is not committed:
  - New Year's Day, with the NYSE's Saturday rule: no Friday close when 1 January is a Saturday;
  - MLK Day (NYSE closed for it from 1998);
  - Presidents' Day;
  - Good Friday (Easter computus);
  - Memorial Day;
  - Juneteenth (from 2022);
  - Independence Day and Christmas (Saturday → Friday, Sunday → Monday);
  - Labor Day;
  - Thanksgiving.
- **Unscheduled closures listed explicitly:**
  - 2001-09-11 to 09-14 (the attacks);
  - 2004-06-11 (Reagan);
  - 2007-01-02 (Ford);
  - 2012-10-29 and 10-30 (Sandy);
  - 2018-12-05 (G. H. W. Bush);
  - 2025-01-09 (Carter);
  - any others the sources show.
- **Early closes:** the published NYSE history is the reference, cross-checked against the open-source `exchange_calendars` XNYS calendar. I'll read its source on GitHub, not install it. It encodes the day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, the days around 4 July, and the pre-2004 special cases with citations. Each pre-2004 half day gets a comment with its source.
- **The cross-check that settles holidays, against the real exports:** every day from 1999-01-22 to 2026-11-06 is compared with `BATS_NVDA, 1D.csv` (6,992 daily bars), and 2010-06-29 onward with `BATS_TSLA, 1D.csv`.
  - A day we call a session must have a bar, and a day with a bar must be a session. The target is zero mismatches either way.
  - Any mismatch is investigated and brought to you, never forced.
  - The exports stay in your Downloads folder; they are read only, and nothing is copied into the repo (licensing, OI-27).
- **1999-01-01 to 01-21, and 2026-11-07 to 2027-12-31,** lie outside the exports. They rely on the rules and published calendars. NYSE has published its 2027 holidays and early closes, and I'll check them against nyse.com in Chrome.

**3. Code changes (data only):**
- `NYSE_HOLIDAYS` and `NYSE_EARLY_CLOSES` gain 1999–2019 and 2027, with a comment on each unscheduled closure.
- `coveredYears` becomes [1999, 2027].
- The stale "needs a human check" warning is replaced with what each range was validated against.

- **a rule check:** every year's scheduled holidays from 1999 to 2027 equal the rule computation, plus the explicit closures list. A typo in the lists fails;

- the existing 2020–2026 tests stay unchanged;
- a test that compares with `nvda_1d.csv` when that file is present in the exports folder, skipped (with a printed reason) until OI-27 places the stock exports.

**5. `docs/bar-rules.md`:** the holidays line says the calendar covers 1999–2027 and how it was validated.

**Out of scope:** stock D/W/M cooking (M1-08), and any change to session or time-zone logic. Pre-2007 DST is already handled by tzdata, per the M0-04 tests.
