Part 1 (this PR), in `contexts/indicators/src/domain`:
1. `settings.ts`: `checkSettings(inputs, given)` refuses undeclared keys, wrong types, fractions for whole numbers, values outside min..max and options not on the list, with a reason per field; missing keys take their default. `settingsKey` gives a stable text form for the engine's cache key (M3-04).
2. `settings-dialog.ts`: `settingsDialog(definition, current?)` describes the dialog as plain JSON, one field per input (number box with limits and step, switch, or drop-down), with current values and defaults.

Part 2 (after M2-05): a React dialog in the chart app that draws `SettingsDialog`, and the route that receives settings calls `checkSettings` and returns its errors as a 400.
