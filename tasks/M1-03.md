## Plan
Approved by Sham on 2026-11-09: option A (`tsx` as a root devDependency) and the clipped window.

**Our bars come from** the cooked store (`CookedBarStorePort`, MinIO bucket `aia-cooked`) for 5m to 1M, and for 1m from hydration's raw store (`@aia/hydration`, bucket `aia-raw`, provider `coinapi` for crypto), read day by day through `OneMinuteBarSourcePort`. Both are empty today, so every file reports NOT BUILT and the run exits 1.

**Layers, all in `contexts/market-data/src`:**
1. `domain/parity.ts` (pure): `compareBars` matches by time and compares OHLCV exactly as scaled integers. It returns matched, differing, missing, extra and the first difference (both rows plus the fields that differ). It also works out the window.
2. `application/run-parity.ts`: per instrument and timeframe, load the fixture, read our bars over its range, clip, compare. Every reader is a port.
3. `adapters/parity-report.ts`: the summary table and the first-difference blocks.
4. `adapters/parity-cli.ts`: wires MinIO and the loader. Flags: `--symbol`, `--timeframe`, `--fixtures`. Exits 0 only when every compared file is exact.
5. Root `package.json`: `"parity": "tsx contexts/market-data/src/adapters/parity-cli.ts"`.

**Window:** from the later of the fixture's first bar and our first *whole* bar, to the fixture's second-to-last bar (the loader drops the forming one). If our history starts after the fixture does, our first bar is skipped, because it may be built from a partial bucket. The report prints the window and how many fixture bars it skipped. Inside the window, a missing bar is a failure.

**Synthetic samples** in `adapters/parity-samples/`: a BTC 1m series and its expected 5m/15m/1h/4h. Tests: cook them, then exact; one wrong bar, then FAIL naming it; empty store, then NOT BUILT and exit 1; plus unit tests for `compareBars` and the window.

**Out of scope:** the CI parity job (M1-13), stocks (OI-27), D/W/M cooking (M1-08).
